# Execution Strategy & Risks

## Execution Strategy
- Launch early with a stable core product.
- Acquire initial users and collect feedback.
- Prioritize improvements based on real customer needs.
- Expand into a complete content operating system through iterative releases.
- Focus on shipping a stable product rather than a perfect one.

## Success Metrics
- 1,000+ active users.
- Agency adoption.
- High user retention.
- Reliable infrastructure.
- Growing subscription revenue.

## Team Responsibilities
- **Engineering:** Build reliable infrastructure and features.
- **Design:** Deliver intuitive user experience.
- **Product:** Prioritize roadmap and validate feedback.
- **Marketing:** Drive launches and user acquisition.
- **Support:** Collect and organize customer feedback.

## Risks
- Feature creep before launch.
- Over-engineering instead of shipping.
- Infrastructure costs.
- Low initial adoption.
